# Goals for the current best cat (from the blind judge, round 1)

KEEP: The pointed ears with pink inner ears, the green eye, the white muzzle and chest block, the warm orange shading lit from the top-left, and the clean outline and bottom edge.

GOALS:
1. The tail should read as one clear shape curling around the front feet, with a visible tip. At 1x it now blends into the haunch and looks like a brown smudge.
2. The back should show 2 or 3 distinct darker tabby stripes. They should read as stripes and not as scattered dark blotches.
3. The head should be easier to tell apart from the body. It needs a clearer face, with the eye, muzzle and nose grouped and a slight neck break, so the cat reads as facing right and not as a blob with a white patch.
4. The front legs and paws should read clearly, with the white chest running down into the feet, and without stray dark pixels near the base.
