# Goals for the current best cat (from the blind judge, round 2)

KEEP: The pointed ears with pink inner ear, the green eye, the white muzzle and chest column, the dark outline, the top-left light on the head and back, and the tail sweeping along the bottom.

GOALS:
1. The tail should be a distinct curl that wraps around the front feet. It should be clearly separate from the body and read as a tail, not a base strip or a shadow. Give it a visible tip.
2. The back and haunch stripes should read as 2 or 3 deliberate tabby bands. Right now the dark brown patches under the body look muddy and merge into the shadow. Keep them clean and rhythmic.
3. The face should read at 1x. The eye and muzzle should be clearer and the white should sit cleanly, with no stray pink or white nub at the nose edge. Add a hint of front paws or a sitting posture at the bottom so it isn't a flat slab.
