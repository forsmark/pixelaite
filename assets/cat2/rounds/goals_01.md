# Goals for the current best cat (from the judge, written as goals, not pixel positions)

What already works and must be kept: the upright sitting pose, the tall head with two pointed pink-lined ears, the green eye, the white muzzle and chest. It reads as a cat at 3x.

Problems, most important first:
1. The tail does not read. The lower back part of the cat is a dark, muddy mass. A player should see a distinct tail that leaves the rump, wraps around the base of the body and ends in front of the front paws.
2. The haunch (the rounded back leg of a sitting cat) is not clear. It should read as a round bulge at the back, lit from the top-left.
3. The tabby stripes are blotches. They should be 2 or 3 thin, clear, evenly spaced stripes across the back.
