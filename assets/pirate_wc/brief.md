# Brief: pirate, watercolor

- Canvas 384x512 (portrait). Medium: watercolor with light ink line work, like a storybook illustration.
- Subject: a pirate character, full body, standing, three-quarter or side view facing RIGHT.
  A tricorn hat, a beard, a long red coat, a belt with a gold buckle, a cutlass held forward,
  one wooden peg leg and one black boot.
- Background: a light, loose suggestion of a ship deck or sea and sky. It must stay quieter than the figure.
- Light from the top-left. Warm light, cool shadows.
- Use: character card art for a cozy pirate game. It must read at 1/4 size.
- Palette: free, but keep it limited (about 5 to 7 pigments) and save white paper for the lightest lights.
