# Brief: steampunk fish, watercolor

- Canvas 512x384 (landscape). Medium: watercolor with light ink line work.
- Subject: a mechanical steampunk fish in side view, facing left. A riveted brass and copper body,
  a round porthole eye, one or two visible gears set into its side, a small exhaust vent or pipe on its back,
  fins and tail made of thin metal plates.
- Background: a loose, pale underwater or murky-sky wash with a few bubbles. Quieter than the fish.
- Light from the top-left. Warm brass highlights (save the paper), cool blue-green shadows on the belly
  and the lower right. Verdigris (green patina) accents on the copper.
- Use: a card or icon in a cozy pirate game. It must read at 1/4 size.
- Palette: limited, about 5 to 6 pigments.

## Animation
- A seamless loop of 8 to 12 frames at about 80 to 110 ms each.
- The tail wags: it rotates as one piece (or two linked pieces) around its base, a few degrees each way.
- The gears turn: each gear rotates as a whole. Meshing gears turn in opposite directions. Pick the turn
  per loop so the last frame flows into the first (for example one tooth pitch per loop, or a full turn).
- Steam comes out of the vent: small puffs appear at the vent, rise, drift, grow and fade, then a new puff
  starts. A light per-frame "boil" on the steam is fine.
- The body may bob 1 to 2 px. The background stays still. Nothing else may flicker.
